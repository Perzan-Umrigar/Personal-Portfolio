Case Study — Project 02

VEX Robotics — Founding Team

Founding Member & Lead Driver · Grade 12
🏆 City Champions, VEX Robotics Tournament (founding year)

Overview

St. Mary's College had no VEX Robotics program until this year. As a founding member of the team, I helped build a competitive robotics program — and a competition robot — from essentially nothing: no prior team experience, no inherited parts inventory, and no returning members to learn from.

Objectives

  • Establish the school's first VEX Robotics team and competition program
  • Design, build, and wire a functional VEX competition robot from scratch
  • Write the robot's autonomous-round code and core function programming
  • Develop and execute competitive driving strategy in live matches

Process

01

Building from nothing

With no existing club, no hand-me-down parts, and no upperclassmen to learn from, the team taught itself VEX robot design and programming from the ground up — largely through trial, error, and self-directed research.

02

Resourceful part sourcing

When the design called for a part we didn't have, we adapted what we already had on hand — reshaping and repurposing old or spare components to fit, rather than waiting on parts we couldn't source in time.

03

Programming

Wrote the autonomous-mode code framework that ran the robot's opening routine without driver input, along with the core functions — drivetrain control and mechanism actuation — used throughout every match.

04

Driving role & strategy

Selected as lead driver based on having the most hands-on experience and mechanical familiarity with the robot on the team. Because the robot's design was simpler than many competitors', driving precision and in-match strategy carried a significant share of the results.

Outcomes

✓
City Champions — VEX Robotics Tournament, founding year of the team
✓
Proved that a first-year team with no prior experience could still be a strong contender
✓
Set a result for future team members to build on — a win, and a target to strive for

The win meant our team wasn't just new — it was already a contender, and it gave the members who come after us something to chase.
